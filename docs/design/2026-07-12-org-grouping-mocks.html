The lens — org grouping, round 2

Round 1 killed the scope toggle (D) and picked owner clustering (B). The pushback that reopened it: same GitHub account on both laptops. At work I want the org leading. At home I want my own repos leading. But some evenings I still want to know whether anything new landed at work — without giving the work set my whole island.

That's not one want, it's two — and D failed because it served both with a single mode. A durable lens (which context leads on this machine) and a transient check ("anything new over there?") need different verbs. Round 2: keep the group-by, and let the secondary group fold, not filter — it compresses to one counted caption line in the island's existing (show)/(hide) grammar. Nothing leaves the glass untraced, so the doctrine objection dissolves.

Why the fold fixes what the filter broke: a filter removes rows and the pill keeps counting them — a lie. A fold prints the count it hides ("4 at acme, 1 new") — compression, not omission. The audit trail stays on the glass. Space is the scarce resource; honesty is the non-negotiable one. The fold spends the first without touching the second.

Rules, each earned by a story moment

  • Lens = checkable set (Mon 21:40): checked owners lead, unchecked fold — a set of one reads like a radio, so the two-owner desk never meets the checkbox.
  • One caption for the whole complement (Mon 10:04): "3 elsewhere" — collapsed cost is one line at any org count; per-org captions rejected for scaling clutter.
  • Caption names the org when the complement is one owner ("3 at acme, 1 new") and when one org has the news ("4 elsewhere, 1 new at acme"); several with news → count only, names move to the expansion's sub-headers (Sun 20:15).
  • Discovered, not configured: the menu lists owners present in your rows plus "yours", rebuilt live on open (the Surface submenu's menuNeedsUpdate precedent); an org with no open PRs quietly leaves; a checked owner with zero rows degrades honestly.
  • Within the lens set, B's clustering orders owners; A's elision applies only to yours — helios-oss keeps its name on the row (Mon 21:40, third row).
  • One (hide) rides the fold's top-right corner — one region, one verb, however many sub-headers inside.

Mechanics, both variants

  • One pref: githud.lens.owners (a set) in UserDefaults — per-machine by nature, no sync, no schedule, no "work hours" heuristics to get wrong. Empty set = no lens = B.
  • The peek is PeekReveal's existing session-scoped machinery, not the sticky gear-pref flip — a deliberate asymmetry with the stale/bots captions, same as the drafts no-caption asymmetry. The lens pref is the only durable truth.
  • Caption strings join PlainWords.swift: lensCaption(count:owners:fresh:) → one folded owner: "3 at acme, 1 new (show)" · "2 of yours (show)"; several: "4 elsewhere, 1 new at acme (show)"; spoken forms mirror the existing pattern.
  • "1 new" = arrivals since this machine last expanded the group — rides the D1 per-fact stale-clock plumbing, no new polling.
  • Space math: each folded row returns ~34px; the lane caps at 240px (~6 rows), so folding a 3-PR work set is the difference between your own queue living above the fold or under a scrollbar.
  • The pill never changes: H1-only, and the lens never touches H1. Folded rows stay counted everywhere counts appear.
  • When Inbound widens to org repos (the SignalClassifier.swift:122 future-config), the same lens governs it for free — the caption grammar is already the lane's native idiom.

Round 4 — the actual proposal, everything composed · live

F: titled groups, and the eye lives on the titles

The session asked two closing questions: which one is actually proposed? and can the eye coexist with the sub-section titles? One answer serves both. The sub-headers are the structure of any mixed lane — always on, and rows beneath them drop the owner prefix because the title said it. Each title carries its own eye at the right edge: shut an owner's eye and that group folds durably — and here's the join — the folded group's title line is the caption: "acme · 3, 1 new". The lens is no longer a separate control with a card; it's the set of open eyes, edited in place.

Ratified with two amendments (round 5): the eye made (show) redundant — the verb is gone, and with it the peek state (opening is durable; the Sunday check is open-look-shut, two clicks on the same spot). And eyes are hover-revealed within their group's region — the control is chrome-silent at rest, because the honesty never lived in the eye: the folded count line is persistent ink, so the lane always admits what's hidden. Reveal is forgiving (the whole folded line opens the group); folding is deliberate (only the eye itself hides rows). Hover a group below to meet its eye. Round 6 revised this composition — the session returned to E3's single eye and added the grouping-vs-now toggle to its card; F's other grammar carries over. See the final round below.

The spec, in six lines

  • Mixed lane → owner sub-headers, always. Rows drop the owner/ prefix (the title said it); repo names stay. Single-owner lane → no titles, no eyes (lone-header guard). "Needs you" exempt, as ever.
  • The eye rides each title's right edge, hover-revealed within its group's region (and on keyboard focus). Shut = that owner folds on this desk (durable, per-machine). Open = leads. The lens is the set of open eyes — direct manipulation, no picker card, and zero chrome at rest.
  • A folded group's title is its ledger line: "acme · 3, 1 new" — persistent ink, so the lane admits what's hidden even while the eye is invisible. No (show), no peek state: the whole folded line opens the group (reveal is forgiving, full-width target); only the eye itself folds one (hiding is deliberate, precise target). The Sunday check is open-look-shut — two clicks on the same spot.
  • Two grammars coexist by scope: the eye is org-grouping's control; the stale / bots & drafts / drafts families keep their ratified word-captions. Open mic: converge them later if dogfood wants one grammar.
  • Group order: open groups first, by their lead row's rank; folded lines sink to the lane's bottom. Beyond two folded owners they merge into one "elsewhere · N (show)" line — the tail safety valve.
  • Gear stays the config twin: "Lead with" checkboxes flip the same eyes (two handles, one truth). Pill untouched; folded rows stay counted everywhere counts appear.

What each round contributed — and what dissolved

  • A's elision → survives, carried by the titles. B's clustering → survives as group order. C's partitions → survive as the titles themselves, now load-bearing.
  • E1's picker card → dissolved (direct manipulation); its gear item survives as the twin. E2's (lead) verb → dissolved. E3's lane-header eye → moved down one level, onto the titles, where it gained a group to govern.
  • Round 3's lane-bottom combined caption → dissolved into the folded title lines; survives only as the >2-folded-owners merge.
  • Round 5, at ratification: F's own (show)/(hide) and the peek state → dissolved — the eye is the one control, the count line is the honesty, and eyes went hover-quiet.

Round 6 — ratified revision · live

One eye, one card — and the way back to now

Working the F mock, the session came back for E3's single eye — and asked for the one thing its card was missing: below a separator, a toggle between grouping and now. That's the escape hatch the whole feature needed. Org grouping is a view, and a view you can try must have a one-click way back: uncheck Group by owner and the lane returns to today's shape — one state-sorted list, no titles. The lens applies in both views (session amendment): unchecked owners stay folded either way — in the grouped view their ledger line sits where the group would; in the flat view it sits at the lane's foot. The toggle changes the shape of what leads, never what's hidden — so it's still a fold, not a filter, in both. (A's quiet typography stays either way — "now" means structure, not ink.) The trade against F, stated plainly: per-group eyes go, so folding an owner happens in the card rather than on its title — in exchange the titles return to pure text, the lane has one control point, and the two-grammar worry closes. F's forgiving reveal survives in both views: clicking a folded ledger line opens that owner. Hover the lane, click the eye, try all four combinations.

The final spec — what round 6 changes, what carries

  • The eye lives on the lane header, hover-revealed within the lane (and on keyboard focus): slashed while grouped-and-folding, open otherwise. One eye per foldable lane (Your PRs; Inbound when org scope lands).
  • Its card is the whole lens: "Lead with:" checkable owners, then a separator, then Group by owner. The two are orthogonal: the toggle changes shape (titled groups vs one flat state-sorted list with prefixes back on rows), the checkboxes change who leads — and they apply in both shapes. Folded owners keep their ledger line either way; in the flat view it sits at the lane's foot (where folded lines sink in the grouped view too — same rule). Two prefs: githud.lens.groupByOwner + githud.lens.owners; the gear mirrors both (two handles, one truth).
  • Sub-headers return to pure text — no per-title eyes. Folding an owner happens in the card; opening stays forgiving: the folded ledger line ("acme · 3, 1 new") is still a full-width click target.
  • Everything else carries from F: folded lines as persistent honesty ink, no (show)/peek, open-groups-first order, >2-folded merge valve, owner elision under titles, radar and pill untouched, word-captions stay with the stale/bots families.

Decide

Ratified — round 6

Ratified 2026-07-14, round 6: E3's single lane-header eye, its card carrying the lens ("Lead with:" checkable owners) and, below a separator, the Group by owner toggle — the one-click switch between the grouped view and the lane as it is now. Amended same day: the lens is view-independent — the toggle changes shape, never visibility; folded owners keep their ledger line at the flat lane's foot too. F's per-group eyes traded for the one control point; all other F grammar carries (folded ledger lines as honesty ink, no (show), hover-quiet chrome, forgiving open on folded lines, order rules, radar and pill untouched). Rounds 4–5 stay in this page as the road. Next: the usual gauntlet — builder in worktree, three-lens panel, re-verify, land.

Considered and rejected, recorded: compressed half-height rows for the secondary group (half-attention rendering is chrome pretending to be a decision — the caption's count says as much in one line); time-of-day auto-lens (a schedule guessing at attention is a mode you didn't set); per-org accent tinting (color stays CI vocabulary).
