githud · WP V3 · architecture + program design

Gone quiet joins the tail

The owner lens governs live rows and drafts. It does not govern gone quiet — so folding an org leaves its rotting PRs on screen. This closes the last org-blind region and makes the fold mean what it says.

Ratified 2026-07-29 (dogfood): "in lens i disabled 'yours' … in gone quiet and draft PRs, i see other orgs still" → tail it is; both regions tail the org; order rows → drafts → quiet (descending relevance, not inherited region order); copy stays "gone quiet" on glass and stale in code.
0 · Brief

The claim, the decision, and what is still open

The claim. Folding an owner in the lens currently hides its live rows and counts them on a ledger line, but its gone quiet rows keep rendering in a flat region below — org-blind, unaffected by the fold. On the reporting lane every stale PR belonged to the one folded owner, so folding "yours" emptied the live glance and left that section as the only populated thing on screen. The mechanism: the lens's domain widens from two regions to three, so each owner group ends with its own drafts and then its own quiet, and a folded owner's ledger line admits all three counts.

The decision, and what it beat. LensEntry.group gains a third payload and lensLayout starts returning a LensLayout value instead of a bare array. The rejected alternative was a general [Region: [PulseRow]] map on the group — attractive because a fourth region would then be free. It loses because the three regions are not interchangeable at the view: drafts carry a count label and no control (the ratified no-caption asymmetry), quiet carries a collapsed caption that is its affordance, and live rows carry neither. A uniform map still needs a hand-written branch per region for its chrome, so it buys no consumer collapse — and it invites summing regions uniformly, which is exactly the mistake the live-only fresh rule was written to avoid.

Non-goals and open questions. No per-owner reveal state — clicking any group's (show) flips the one lane-wide showStale pref, as the gear already does. No change to what counts as stale (staleAfter stays 14 days). Open: whether an org whose only rows are 16 weeks old deserves a title and a fold — this design says yes for consistency with the draft-only precedent, and names it as the thing to watch. Open: whether a four-clause ledger line still scans. Assumed: that extending the dim treatment to quiet rows reads as calm rather than as broken — it changes flat-shape appearance too, and only glass will settle it.

1 · The load-bearing decision

The two prefs gate at different layers, and that is not an inconsistency

Correction to the earlier plan

The per-org-drafts plan said V3 would simply add showStale: beside showDrafts: on lensRegions. That is wrong, and the reason is doctrine, not convenience.

showDrafts gates the lens's INPUT. Drafts have no collapsed caption — the ratified asymmetry is that hidden drafts stay fully invisible. So "hidden" and "absent" mean the same thing, and passing [] is exactly right: it also stops a folded ledger from claiming drafts the pref is hiding lane-wide.

showStale gates the group's RENDERING. Quiet's whole grammar is that it leaves a count behind — "2 gone quiet (show)" is both the honesty and the affordance. Gate its input and a stale-only owner disappears along with its count, which breaks fold-not-filter. So stale enters the lens unconditionally and the view chooses caption versus rows.

PrefGatesBecauseConsequence
showDraftslens input no collapsed caption ⇒ hidden = absent draft-only owner vanishes when off; ledger drops its draft clause
showStalegroup rendering caption ⇒ hidden = counted stale-only owner always gets a group; ledger always carries its quiet clause

Invariants

  • Fold, not filter (three regions). Every input row — live, draft, or quiet — appears exactly once: a visible row, a tail row, a flat terminal row, or inside a ledger count. In both shapes, under every pref combination.
  • The tails are positional. Within a group, drafts then quiet render after rows and never join their state sort. This is the 2026-06-18 inversion guard, now restated for two tails.
  • Walk order == render order. Extended to the quiet tail, and to the flat terminal reorder.
  • Ledger honesty. A folded owner's line counts live, drafts, and quiet — clause suppressed at zero, never · 0.
  • Four surfaces, one truth. Lane groups, ledger lines, the Owners card, and the lane eye agree on the owner set and its order — now over three regions.

Program obligations

Discharged into declarations in section 2; each is marked at its landing site.

  • O1  The per-owner partition takes three regions, and OwnerBucket is the one place their per-owner split is expressed.
  • O2  lensLayout returns the whole layout — entries plus both terminal sets plus the shape — so no consumer can compute one and forget the other. The free functions that exist to rebuild that answer go private.
  • O3  Row de-emphasis is derived from the row, never passed: one predicate meaning "not in the live glance", covering both tails and both shapes.

7 · Scope, risk, disconfirming evidence

What this does not do, and what would prove it wrong

Scope boundaries

  • No per-owner reveal state. One lane-wide showStale; any group's caption flips it for all. A second persisted set is not in scope.
  • No change to the stale rule. staleAfter stays 14 days; the threshold stays in the gear tooltip.
  • The pill, the radar, the H1 queue, polling, persistence are untouched.
  • Deferred: scroll-to-reveal on a pref flip — still open from 2026-07-26, and a third tail makes the 240pt pane cap bite harder, not less.
  • Deferred: whether tail rows keep GitHub's CI badge or take the ratified mock's neutral WIP glyph — unanswered from V2, and now it would apply to two tails.
