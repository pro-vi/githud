Mechanics, all flavors

  • The caption is one full-width click target (like every island row) — not just the underlined verb.
  • Clicking flips the same preference the gear items flip; the revealed header's hide flips it back. No second state to drift.
  • The 14-day threshold leaves the glass but not the app: it moves to the gear item's tooltip, where a curious user finds it.
  • Reveal rides the island's existing grammar — 150ms height, 120ms ink, 0ms under Reduce Motion. No new animation vocabulary.
  • VoiceOver: the caption is a button ("2 more from bots and drafts, show"); the revealed header's hide control speaks likewise.
