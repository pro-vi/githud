The three open questions, closed

Does facebook belong in the lens card?

The Owners card is built from active rows, so a draft-only owner has never appeared in it. Under V1 facebook gets a lane group — but can you lead with it, fold it, drag it?

Yes — a first-class org. The card is built from live + draft owners. Two knock-ons: your drag order outranks the sink rule (a placed draft-only owner can lead), and a zero-live owner's ledger prints the tail alone — "facebook · 1 draft", never "facebook · 0, 1 drafts".

Does the folded ledger count drafts?

Fold-not-filter says a folded owner always prints its count. Today that count is live rows only. "acme · 4" while 5 drafts hide inside it is arguably a lie.

Yes — two facts, one line: "acme · 4, 5 drafts". Taken as the honest default; it's a copy change, reversible on dogfood.

Does the showDrafts pref survive?

A per-org tail is small and self-labelling. The default-off pref existed to keep WIP out of the glance — a dimmed 2-row tail may do that on its own.

Kept, still default-off — now gating the tails rather than a region. Cheap to retire later if the tail proves quiet enough on its own.

Ratified — build from this

The spec, in five lines

  1. The lens takes all three regions, not just active. lensLayout(active:) becomes owner-partitioned over live + drafts; each LensEntry.group carries rows and a separate tail. Group rank still comes from the lead live row.
  2. The tail is positional, never sorted. Drafts sort among themselves (state-then-updated, unchanged) and always render last within the group. This is the 2026-06-18 inversion guard restated at group scope — it wants a pinned test.
  3. A draft-only owner is a first-class lens owner — group with an empty live region (header, then tail), listed in the Owners card, foldable and draggable. Order: ownerOrder wins as always; among unplaced owners the draft-only ones sink below everyone with live work, regardless of recency.
  4. Folding an owner folds its drafts, and the ledger prints both facts — "acme · 4, 5 drafts". A zero-live owner prints the tail alone: "facebook · 1 draft". This is the real win: today, folding acme leaves its 5 drafts on screen, which quietly breaks fold-not-filter.
  5. "Draft PRs" as a region retires, along with its revealed header and (hide). showDrafts now gates the tails. Stale is untouched — it stays a flat caption, and that asymmetry is the open question V3 answers.
