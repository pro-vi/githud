Record D-morph · feeds WP-3d / WP-3d′ · the signature move

When the pill becomes the island, what is the ink's contract mid-flight?

Three honest answers: the pill's top band hands off into the header (overlap crossfade) · the advancing bottom edge reveals ink already laid out in place · the glass morphs empty and prints its ink after. All three run one ~220ms container morph, radius locked at 14, top edge pinned 8px under the menu bar. Click to judge.

Today

The morph does not exist — expand/collapse is a hard cut: setExpanded → renderNow() removes old content, snaps the panel size, adds new content (HUDPanelController.swift:326-338). There is no NSAnimationContext anywhere in Sources; the app's only animation is the 0.12s hover fade, which already models the Reduce Motion contract. Hide/show is also a hard cut; while hidden, the static status-item glyph is the only handle back. All three variants degrade to exactly today's cut under Reduce Motion, and all are interruptible mid-flight.

Hand-off — the pill grows into the header

morph-hand-off-header runner's pickamend

One continuous surface morphs on a single curve while the pill's top band crossfades into the expanded header in place — the pill is revealed to have been the island's header all along. No empty-glass beat.

Build spec
EXPAND 220ms, ONE NSAnimationContext.runAnimationGroup, cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0,1), width+height on the same curve simultaneously; radius constant 14. Expanded content built at final size immediately, added at opacity 0. Pill ink pinned in the top band, fades 1→0 over 0–100ms; header fades 0→1 over 80–200ms; body + footer fade 0→1 over 120–220ms. No per-row stagger, no text translation — the hand-off is the top BAND, not a traveling glyph. COLLAPSE 180ms mirrored (body out 0–70, header out 0–100, pill ink in 100–180). HIDE: collapse first, then pill fades out with 8px upward tuck (the menuBarGap), 140ms; status item stays a static handle — no glyph change, no flash. SHOW: fade in with 8px downward settle, 140ms. REDUCE MOTION: skip every group → today's hard cut. INTERRUPTION: single simultaneous curve retargets trivially from presentation values. Shadow: invalidateShadow() once, in the completion handler.
Doctrine fit & risks
Fit: motion maps 1:1 to the summon click; one continuous object — nothing appears from nowhere; opacity-only ink survives gray-swap; no stagger, no overshoot; the band hand-off crossfades real content at real positions — no fake traveling glyph.

Risks: present() must keep the outgoing pill ink alive through flight (today it's removed synchronously — the main build delta). The 80–200ms overlap can read as brief double-exposure in the top band — judge at real size. Hide's collapse-then-fade is a 320ms two-beat; the pre-registered fallback is cutting straight to the fade.
Refuter — amend: doctrine-clean on every hunted axis; the one violation was in the mock: a pill count of 3 beside 7 visible rows — a state the code can never produce (both bind to rows.count).
pill count, header badge, and row count must agree — applied to all mocks on this page (7 everywhere).

Unfurl — width leads, the edge reveals

morph-unfurl-edge-reveal amend

Dynamic-Island-style two-axis choreography: the pill snaps wide first, then the bottom edge unfurls downward, revealing rows already laid out in their final places — content never fades or moves; the real edge is the only performer.

Build spec
EXPAND 240ms, one transaction, two phases: width 60→520 over 0–120ms cubic-bezier(0.3,0,0,1); height 36→H over 60–240ms cubic-bezier(0.32,0.72,0,1) — spring-flavored decel, zero overshoot. Content built and framed at FINAL size at t=0, clipped by the growing container; zero opacity animation on the body. Only crossfade: pill ink out 0–60ms, header in 60–140ms. COLLAPSE 200ms reversed (height first, then width). Doctrine fit & risks
Fit: the purest never-fabricate content contract — rows exist at final positions from t=0 and a REAL edge reveals them; the bottom fade doubles as the overflow affordance, so mid-flight and at-rest meaning are identical.

morph-glass-then-ink keep

What you're deciding

Runner's pick: hand-off. It delivers the signature single-object morph the PLAN asks for, retargets trivially when interrupted, and avoids the empty-glass flicker. Ratifying a morph here also ratifies that fade behavior (static at rest, removed on scroll-to-end) — decide it once, knowingly. Build burden either way: the scroll-fade listener must be event-driven and live only while expanded (idle-footprint).
